Mainland License

A Mainland license permits full market access across Dubai and the UAE, including direct trade with the local economy, eligibility for government tenders, and seamless logistics and banking.

Direct Local Market Access:
Conduct business freely across the entire UAE market and with government entities.
No Location Restrictions:
Establish your office, warehouse, or retail space anywhere within Dubai.
Government Contract Eligibility:
Qualify to bid for contracts with UAE government departments.
Unrestricted Business Activity:
Operate with a wide range of commercial, industrial, and professional activities.
Simplified Logistics & Banking:
Enjoy straightforward shipping, customs clearance, and local banking processes.

Freezone License

A UAE freezone license offers 100% foreign ownership, full repatriation of profits, and exemption from corporate and personal income taxes, with streamlined setup and world class infrastructure.

  • Dubai Multi Commodities Centre (DMCC) – Global hub for commodities/trade.
  • Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC) – Premier financial hub.
  • Dubai Airport Freezone (DAFZA) – Aviation-linked businesses.
  • Dubai Silicon Oasis (DSO) – Tech and innovation.
  • Dubai Healthcare City (DHCC) – Medical and wellness.
  • Dubai Media City (DMC) / Dubai Internet City (DIC) – Media, tech, ICT.
  • Dubai South – Aviation/logistics hub.
  • Dubai Design District (d3) – Design/fashion/art.

Abu Dhabi

  • Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM) – International financial free zone.

  • Masdar City – Clean-tech and sustainable companies.

Sharjah
